## Is Your Plumbing Trying to Tell You Something?

If you have a slow-draining sink or gurgling toilet it might be a warning sign that there's something wrong inside your pipes. While a lot of folks often go straight for chemical drain cleaner, that often just pushes the problem further down the line.

Please check out these five signs that it's time to call a professional drain and sewer guy right here in NEPA:

### 1. Your Drains Are Slowing Down

This is the most common sign. If your shower, sink, or tub takes longer than usual to drain, it means there's a blockage forming. Soap, hair, grease, kids toys, and other debris may be building up and restricting water flow. Before it becomes a complete clog, it's wise to get it cleaned professionally.

### 2. Gurgling Sounds

If you happen to hear gurgling noises from your drains after you flush a toilet or run the sink then there's trapped air in your pipes. The air is being forced through a partial clog and creating that sound. This is a clear indicator of a blockage that needs to be addressed.

### 3. Unpleasant Odors Are Coming from the Drains

Do you smell a foul, sewer-like odor coming from your drains? That's often caused by trapped food particles and other gunk decomposing inside your pipes. A professional cleaning will not only get rid of the smell, but fix the clog and save your pipes.

### 4. You Have Frequent Clogs

If you're constantly using a plunger to fight with clogs in your toilet or drain, it means there's a deeper issue that a home tools can't solve. A professional "sewer guy" can use a camera to inspect the line, find the root cause, and clear it for good.

### 5. Water Is Backing Up

This is the most serious sign. If water is backing up out of a drain (especially a floor drain in the basement) when you use another fixture, you likely have a blockage in your main sewer line. This is a plumbing emergency that requires immediate attention to prevent property damage—it could quickly get worse.
