## Is Your Plumbing Trying to Tell You Something?

That slow-draining sink or gurgling toilet isn't just a minor inconvenience. It's a warning sign from deep inside your pipes. While it’s tempting to grab a chemical drain cleaner, this often just pushes the problem further down the line.

Here are five signs that it's time to call a professional drain and sewer guy in the Scranton, PA area.

### 1. Your Drains Are Slowing Down

This is the most common sign. If your shower, sink, or tub takes longer than usual to drain, it means a blockage is forming. Soap scum, hair, grease, and other debris are building up, restricting water flow. Before it becomes a complete clog, it's wise to get it professionally cleaned.

### 2. You Hear Gurgling Sounds

Strange gurgling noises from your drains after you flush a toilet or run the sink mean there's trapped air in your pipes. The air is being forced through a partial clog, creating that unsettling sound. It's a clear indicator of a blockage that needs to be addressed.

### 3. Unpleasant Odors Are Coming from the Drains

Can you smell a foul, sewer-like odor from your drains? That's often caused by trapped food particles and other gunk decomposing inside your pipes. A professional cleaning doesn't just mask the smell—it removes the source.

### 4. You Have Frequent Clogs

If you're constantly battling clogs in the same drain, it means there's a deeper issue that a simple snake or plunger can't solve. A professional "sewer guy" can use a camera to inspect the line, find the root cause, and clear it for good.

### 5. Water Is Backing Up

This is the most serious sign. If water is backing up out of a drain (especially a floor drain in the basement) when you use another fixture, you likely have a blockage in your main sewer line. This is a plumbing emergency that requires immediate attention to prevent property damage.
